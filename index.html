Our Story

NovaDeskOnline was born out of a simple but powerful observation: people in Kemera and the surrounding Nyamira County had limited access to affordable, professional cyber services. Long distances to town centres, unreliable service, and high prices left many — especially students, small businesses, and families — struggling to handle everyday digital tasks.

In 2024, we decided to change that. NovaDeskOnline was founded to bring high-quality printing, scanning, CV writing, government services, and much more directly to the community — at prices that make sense for everyday Kenyans.

Today, NovaDeskOnline serves students, job seekers, entrepreneurs, schools, hospitals, and NGOs — offering 41+ services under one roof, backed by fast broadband and a team that genuinely cares about its community.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To deliver fast, affordable, and reliable digital services that empower individuals, students, and businesses in rural Kenya to thrive in the digital economy.

Vision: A Kemera where no one is left behind — where every student can print assignments, every entrepreneur can register a business, and every family can access government services without travelling far or paying too much.
